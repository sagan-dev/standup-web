Standup - Desk Remote Control

Your sit/stand desk, one click away in the menu bar — and it stands up before your meetings.

Download on the Mac App Store Open-source edition

Everything a desk remote should do

Simple by default. The automation is there when you want it and quiet when you don't.

Menu bar popover

Height, connection status and desk name at a glance. It lives in the menu bar — no Dock icon, no extra windows.

Hold to move

Hold the round raise or lower button and the desk keeps moving; let go and it stops. It never runs away.

Stand and Sit presets

One click sends the desk to your saved standing or sitting height and stops there. Click again to stop on the way.

Automation

Stands up before meetings

Standup reads your Apple Calendar — including Outlook, Exchange and Microsoft 365 accounts added to macOS — and raises the desk shortly before an online meeting you accepted. It goes back down when the meeting ends.

Automation

Heads-up nudge

Before the desk moves on its own it gives two tiny nudges in the direction it is about to travel, then waits a second — it never starts without a warning.

Optional

Knows when a call runs over

Optionally keeps the desk up while Zoom, Teams, Webex, Slack, FaceTime or your browser is still using the microphone or speaker. It only sees that audio is in use — never the audio itself.

Optional

Unplanned calls

When a call starts that is not in your calendar, a notification offers to raise the desk. Nothing moves until you click.

Optional

Hourly stand goal

Set how many minutes per hour you want to stand. Time you already stood — in meetings or by hand — counts, so the desk only rises for what is missing.

AppleScript and Shortcuts

Scriptable with simple commands, so you can move the desk from Shortcuts, Alfred or your own scripts.

Requirements

  • macOS 12 or newer
  • A Bluetooth sit/stand desk with a Linak controller, such as the IKEA IDÅSEN
  • Bluetooth access (to talk to the desk); Calendar access only if you use the meeting features
